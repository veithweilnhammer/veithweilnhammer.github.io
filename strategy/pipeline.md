## Overlap decisions

- **The Tell / Oldest Form of Mind-Reading / MAILA:** one flagship conceptual
  essay plus one technical project page. Do not publish two essays that both
  traverse Darwin, movement, and latent state without a sharper division.
- **Loneliness / Agreeable Friend / Empathy / Anthropomorphism / ELIZA:** this
  is a cluster, not a five-week release plan. Select pieces by distinct
  mechanism and evidence; avoid sounding like a general companion-AI critic.
- **SIM-VAIL / Counting Harm / Regulate by Use:** keep separate and cross-link.
  They answer different questions: how to detect risk, how to count outcomes,
  and how to govern use.
- **DSM / Diagnosis:** keep separate but space them apart. One describes a
  scientific-institutional shift; the other analyzes how thresholds encode
  decisions.
- **Turing / Prediction:** lead with "Prediction Is Not Understanding." It is
  more distinctive and already has a concrete editorial review.
