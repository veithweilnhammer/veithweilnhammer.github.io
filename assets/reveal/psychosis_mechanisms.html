How can we connect chatbots to mechanisms?

Decode psychological processes from digital behavior, then test how chatbot responses shape them and how mechanism-informed policies can help.
Proposed research programme from the Munich application: pair digital behavior with independent psychological and neural measurements, decode psychological profiles, and test reciprocal influences between chatbot responses and psychological processes through simulations and randomized experiments. This is a conceptual framework, not a result demonstrating that digital decoding or chatbot intervention is already validated.
