How well do chatbots recognize psychiatric emergencies?

Chatbots miss more psychiatric emergencies in conversation.
Inference anchoring: Early judgements persist as new facts lose influence.
Context-seeking collapse: Questions become repetitive and follow narrow patterns linked to triage errors.
Three take-aways. Emergency under-triage rises when the case unfolds over multiple turns. As the conversation continues, new information has less influence and previous dispositions have more. Chatbots also repeat question categories more often than clinicians, and the resulting question patterns are associated with different triage errors.
